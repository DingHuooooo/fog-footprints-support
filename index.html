Common questions

Why does the map show no footprints?

Fog Footprints can only use photos that are visible to the app and contain capture-location metadata. Check Photos access in iOS Settings, then open Settings in the app and choose Update Footprints.

Why are some photos grouped under Unknown Place?

A photo can contain GPS coordinates that do not match a supported land or marine boundary with enough confidence. The app keeps that photo under Unknown Place rather than assigning an incorrect country or region.

Why can a group have photos but show no thumbnails?

This can happen after the visible Photos selection changes or when a photo is stored only in iCloud. Update the footprint after changing Photos access. Thumbnail requests do not download from iCloud; opening an individual photo preview can let the system Photos service retrieve a display-sized image.

How does limited Photos access work?

The footprint is calculated only from the photos you selected. You can change the visible selection in iOS Settings, then update the footprint from the app.

Does the app track my current location?

No. Fog Footprints does not request Core Location permission and does not perform live or background location tracking. It only reads location metadata already stored in photos you allow it to see.

How do I remove local app data?

Open Settings in Fog Footprints and choose Delete All Local Data. This removes the app’s footprints, caches, exclusions, and preferences. It does not delete photos or change the Photos permission managed by iOS.

Privacy by design

On-device processing

Footprint calculation and boundary matching happen on your iPhone.

No account or analytics

The app has no account system, ads, analytics service, or developer cloud sync.

No photo uploads

Photos, thumbnails, locations, footprints, and statistics are not sent to the developer.
