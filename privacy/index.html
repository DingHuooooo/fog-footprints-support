Privacy Policy

How the app processes photo metadata, stores local data, and protects your privacy.

Effective July 22, 2026 · Last updated July 31, 2026

Contents Data processed Photos and thumbnails On-device processing Storage and retention Deletion and permissions Limited access System services No collection Contact

1. Data processed by the app

After you grant Photos access and start a scan, the app may read:

  • Local identifiers of photos currently visible to the app.
  • Photo locations from PHAsset.location.
  • Photo creation and modification dates.
  • Lightweight attributes such as dimensions and media subtype, used to detect changes and reuse local caches.
  • The visible photo count and an identifier fingerprint, used to detect library or limited-access changes.

The app does not request current-location permission, track your location in the background, or perform face, object, text, or scene recognition.

2. Photos and thumbnails

Footprint scanning uses only the metadata above and does not read original photo pixels.

Photo lists, exclusion screens, and point or cluster previews may request local thumbnails through Apple’s Photos framework so you can identify photos. These thumbnails are displayed only on your device and are not uploaded. Thumbnail requests controlled by the app disable iCloud network downloads.

When you explicitly open a full-screen photo preview, the Photos framework may download a display-sized image, including from iCloud. The image remains on the device and is not sent to the developer.

3. On-device processing

The app performs footprint aggregation, offline boundary matching, statistics, map presentation, photo placement, and exclusion processing on your device. It has no account system, advertising, third-party analytics SDK, or developer cloud-sync service.

4. Local storage and retention

The app stores footprint snapshots, exclusions, photo metadata and placement caches, photo-access snapshots, boundary caches, and interface caches in its local container. Application Support data is marked as excluded from device backups.

This data remains until you use Delete All Local Data or uninstall the app. iOS may clear caches earlier; the app can rebuild them from locally available photo metadata and bundled offline boundaries.

5. Deletion and permission control

You can use Settings > Delete All Local Data at any time, including when Photos access is disabled. This removes the app’s footprints, statistics, exclusions, photo metadata, photo placements, access snapshot, language preference, and caches.

Deleting local app data or uninstalling the app does not delete anything from Apple Photos and does not change Photos permission managed by iOS. You can change or revoke that permission separately in System Settings.

6. Limited Photos access

With limited access, the app processes only photos you make visible. The app registers for Photos change notifications only after photo authorization has been determined. Notifications only mark that the library may have changed; the app does not scan in the background. After the app becomes active, it compares authorization and the visible-photo fingerprint and updates footprints only when they actually changed.

7. Network and Apple system services

Footprint generation does not use a developer server. Apple Maps may load basemap content through MapKit. Apple’s Photos service may use the network when you explicitly preview a photo stored only in iCloud. These connections are provided by Apple system services and are subject to Apple’s applicable privacy policies.

8. No collection, tracking, or sale

The app does not transmit photos, thumbnails, photo locations, footprint coordinates, statistics, or local identifiers to the developer or third parties. It therefore does not collect these data under the App Store privacy-label definition. The app does not track you, sell data, or use data for advertising.

9. Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices materially change, this policy will be updated with a new date and explanation.
