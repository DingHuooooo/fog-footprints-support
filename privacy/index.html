Once Here

Privacy Policy

Your photos and footprint data stay on your device.

Effective July 22, 2026 · Last updated July 31, 2026

In short: Once Here has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or developer cloud service. It does not send your photos, locations, footprint data, or statistics to the developer or third parties.

Data the app uses

After you grant Photos access, the app reads only the metadata needed to build your map: a local photo identifier, location, date, and lightweight properties used to detect changes. With Limited Photos access, only the photos you select are processed.

The app does not request current-location permission, track your location in the background, or perform face, object, text, or scene recognition.

Local processing and storage

Map generation, boundary matching, statistics, and exclusions happen on your iPhone. The app keeps map results, preferences, and rebuildable caches in its local container.

Photo thumbnails are requested through Apple Photos only when needed for display. A photo stored in iCloud may download when you explicitly open it. Photos and thumbnails are never sent to the developer.

Network access

The app has no developer server. Apple Maps may load basemap content, and Apple Photos may download an iCloud photo that you choose to open. These connections are handled by Apple’s system services.

Your controls

Use Settings > Delete All Local Data to clear the app’s map data, photo metadata, preferences, and caches. You can also change Photos access in iOS Settings or remove all local app data by uninstalling the app. None of these actions deletes photos from Apple Photos.
